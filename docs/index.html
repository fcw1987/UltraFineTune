Full build & install guide ↗
  1. 1

    Choose the output.

    Select the LG UltraFine in macOS Sound Settings, then select the same output in the app. It must already be your Mac’s default.

  2. 2

    Start tuning manually.

    Open the built app and click Start tuning. Approve system audio capture if macOS asks; tuning begins off on every launch.

  3. 3

    Play something familiar.

    Use comfortable volume and begin with Neutral. Waiting for audio becomes Tuning your audio when processing is ready.

CLEAR BY DESIGN

Local audio. Clear boundaries.

Your audio stays here.

The reviewed implementation processes system playback in memory. No recordings, network transmission, analytics, microphone capture, or screen capture. Tone settings, output preference, and one preset are saved locally.

Privacy details ↗

Native, with a small footprint.

AppKit controls, a Core Audio process tap, a separate output Audio Unit, and in-tree stereo DSP. Apple frameworks power the runtime; no third-party runtime libraries are needed.

RELEASE CANDIDATE, IN PLAIN SIGHT

Know what is verified.

Recorded RC evidence includes an arm64 native build, off-state checks, and automated DSP, ring, callback, and cleanup fixtures. Those results do not establish live sound quality or Apple’s physical audio lifecycle.

  • Live permission, sustained playback, latency, installation, and recovery acceptance remain pending.
  • Stereo default output only; capture and playback need matching nominal sample rates.
  • Protected content and direct output routing may not be captured.
  • Output changes and sleep stop tuning. Confirm the route and restart manually.

BEFORE YOU LISTEN

A few practical answers.

Does it calibrate a specific LG model?

No measured acoustic correction is supplied. Neutral, Everyday, Podcast / Speech, Gaming, Music, Clearer voices, Less boom, and Softer treble are starting points for adjustment by ear. This is an independent project without LG endorsement.

What if tuning is silent or waiting?

Stop tuning, confirm ordinary playback and your default output, and check system audio capture permission. After changing permission, quit and reopen the app. Some protected or directly routed audio may not be captured.

How do I return to ordinary playback?

Click Stop tuning or Quit. Compare original tone only removes the tone adjustment; it keeps trim and headroom. Flat EQ zeroes gains and trim without changing whether processing is running. Live quit, force-quit, disconnect, and sleep recovery still require hardware acceptance.

Can I view this website offline?

Yes. Open index.html in the docs folder. This page uses local CSS and SVG, system fonts, and native HTML controls. It needs no JavaScript, remote fonts, or CDN. Hosting providers may keep their own access logs.
